# Privacy Policy — Birthday

**Last updated:** April 14, 2026

## Overview

Birthday (“the App”) helps you remember birthdays and related notes on your device. This policy describes how information is handled.

## Data we collect

- **Birthday information you enter** — names, dates, notes, groups, gift budgets, and related content you save in the app.
- **No accounts** — the App does not require you to create an account with us.

## How your data is used

- **On your device** — to show your list, reminders, countdowns, cards, and party planning features.
- **Notifications** — if you allow notifications, the system may use your entries to schedule local reminders.
- **Calendar** — if you choose to export or add events, the App uses calendar access only for that action.
- **Microphone & speech** — if you use voice entry, speech is processed to turn your words into text for adding birthdays (as allowed by the system).
- **iCloud** — if you use iCloud, your data may sync across your Apple devices using Apple’s iCloud services, subject to your Apple ID settings.

## Data sharing

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** run third‑party advertising analytics in the App as shipped. Data stays on your devices and, if you enable it, in **your** iCloud account under Apple’s terms.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child provided personal information, contact us (see below).

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
