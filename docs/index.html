Birthday

iPhone app — version 1.0

Never miss a birthday again. Keep people and dates in one place, get reminders before the big day, add entries by voice, and celebrate with cards and simple party planning.

  • Reminders (week before, evening before, and on the day)
  • Countdown, cards, and party guest / to-do lists
  • Optional iCloud sync and Home Screen widget
