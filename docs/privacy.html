Privacy Policy — Birthday

Last updated: April 14, 2026

Overview

Birthday (“the App”) helps you remember birthdays and related notes on your device.

Data we collect

Information you enter: names, dates, notes, groups, gift budgets, and related content. The App does not require an account with us.

How your data is used

  • On your device — to show your list, reminders, countdowns, cards, and party planning.
  • Notifications — if allowed, for local birthday reminders.
  • Calendar — only if you export or add events.
  • Microphone & speech — only for optional voice entry, processed per system speech APIs.
  • iCloud — if enabled, sync across your devices via Apple iCloud under your Apple ID.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The App does not include third‑party advertising analytics as shipped. Data remains on your devices and, if you use it, in your iCloud per Apple’s terms.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change.
